## [medium/CONFIRMED] completeReview raises a mandatory trigger the case does not carry, and can raise one with no fingerprint that nothing can ever clear
WHERE apps/conversation-service/src/run-driver.ts:4908
SCENARIO `outrun` (4908) includes every approved mandatory trigger whose `raisedFingerprints[t]` is undefined. That covers 'never raised' as well as 'raised before P318', and the check never asks whether the trigger applies or is active. Two concrete cases follow.
(a) An adult student (stored DOB 1990-01-01) has a case at AUTHORISED. A reviewer POSTs {outcome:'approved', triggers:['involves_minor'], caseSequence:N}, by slip or by approving both triggers out of habit. `basis.fingerprints.involves_minor` is defined because `reviewFingerprintsOf` hashes the DOB whenever it exists, so 4915 appends HumanReviewRequested{involves_minor} and answers 400. On the next decide `#takeBackFromTheStudent` voids the student's authorisation, the walk pauses for a minor review of an adult, and `documentForWork` refuses every document, because `activeTriggers` now holds involves_minor and so `minorConditions` is set to [].
(b) The reviewer approves ['financial_evidence','involves_minor'] on a case whose financial answer changed and which has NO DOB stored. 4915 raises `triggers: outrun` (both triggers) with `fingerprints: {financial_evidence}` only, so involves_minor is raised with no fingerprint. The domain probe shows that trigger is then uncleared forever. `triggersToRaise` never raises it again (undefined === undefined), `reviewFor` says approvable:false forever, every later approval hits `raise === null` and is refused, and the runbook's 'resolve the stop first so the run raises' never applies. The case is permanently deadlocked and documents are refused.
Before fix #3, an approval of a trigger that had not been raised was a harmless record that cleared nothing. The same 'trigger named without its fingerprint' pattern exists in `#raiseMandatoryTriggers` (4757-4762). It is reachable when an outstanding trigger's answers are gone from the stored profile, for example after a person acts on a P275 deletion request.
FIX In completeReview, only raise triggers that are outstanding or in `basis.applies`; refuse any other approved mandatory trigger without writing anything. Never put a mandatory trigger into a request_human_review's `triggers` without its fingerprint: filter `triggers` to the keys of `fingerprints` in both 4915 and 4762, and treat 'the content it stood on is gone' as its own state, not as a fingerprint-less raising.

## [medium/CONFIRMED] The account hand-back is held behind the re-raised review: the take-back plus the walk toward AUTHORISED pauses hand_over_account before its handoff is raised
WHERE apps/conversation-service/src/run-driver.ts:4650
SCENARIO `hand_over_account` maps to phase handing_over, whose walk target is AUTHORISED (packages/orchestrator/src/durable.ts:133-135). The orchestrator returns it before the authorisation check because the handover is owed whatever the authorisation says (packages/orchestrator/src/run.ts:604-608).
Take a run that has filled (account created, case AUTHORISED by capture) and holds a pre-P318 financial approval, or whose financial or DOB answer changes, and that reaches hand_over_account. `#advanceCase` re-raises the trigger. `#takeBackFromTheStudent` voids the authorisation and moves the case to PREPARING. The next hop toward AUTHORISED, PREPARING -> AWAITING_STUDENT_AUTHORISATION, is refused by the review guard, so `#pauseForReview` runs and the run is escalated. `#raiseHandoff` (4488) runs only after the walk, so the account-handover handoff is never raised until a person approves and resolves the stop.
Before P318 a reviewed case's handover was never held by a review, because the guard counted any past approval. The AUTHORISED-hop skip (4661) does not help: the refusal happens one hop earlier. (If the handoff was already open before the change, the student can still complete it.)
FIX Do not take back or walk the spine for a step whose phase is handing_over, since the hop skip already makes AUTHORISED unreachable for it. Either cap the walk target at the case's current state for hand_over_account, or skip `#takeBackFromTheStudent` and the guarded hops when the step is the account handover, so the handover is raised and the review gates only the student's next yes.
