Three things to take from this beyond the rules.

The grading-system escape is a dead end and the build cannot offer it. Record
it as a row: on Sheffield, choosing "Not in list" for the grading system
leaves a student unable to state their grade, so the offer must not present
that escape as a way through. Whether it should be offered at all with a
plain sentence saying where it leads is yours to propose.

The subject's escape is always available, including on an empty search, which
answers the question your sheet said step 5 would settle.

And the award title's escape submits "", which is why its rule was already
recorded as "when the award title is empty". Worth saying in the entry that
the empty string IS the escape there, so nobody later reads an empty value as
a missing one.
